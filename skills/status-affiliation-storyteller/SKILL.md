# Status & Affiliation Storyteller Skill

## Summary
Craft stories and product experiences that spread naturally by anchoring on human transformation (**Before → After**), peer recognition (**Status**), and community identity (**Affiliation**)—making sharing a benefit to the sharer.

## When to Use
- When writing launch narratives, case studies, README clarity stories, or onboarding flows.
- When users like a product privately but rarely recommend or share it with peers.
- When positioning an ethical, practical, or open-source alternative against entrenched industry habits.

## Steps
1. **Define the Sharp Before / After Transformation:**
   - Map the customer's state across three dimensions:
     - **Practical Reality:** *Before* (3 hours of manual spreadsheet checking and dead links) → *After* (verified answers in 10 seconds).
     - **Emotional State:** *Before* (anxious about hidden traps or inspection surprises) → *After* (calm, prepared, in control).
     - **Identity / Peer Standing:** *Before* (reactive operator putting out fires) → *After* (proactive leader setting the standard).
2. **Engineer Healthy Demand Tension:**
   - Surface the cost of staying with the status quo—not through fear-mongering or dark patterns, but by clearly showing the gap between how things *are* and how clean they *could be*.
   - Let the reader feel the relief of closing that gap.
3. **Map Status Shifts (Beyond Luxury):**
   - Identify how adopting this tool or practice elevates the user's standing in their specific professional circle:
     - *Competence Status:* Being the engineer whose repos never ship broken links or bloated bundles.
     - *Stewardship Status:* Being the kitchen lead or founder who runs a tight, audit-ready operation.
     - *Insider Status:* Knowing the cleaner, smarter way to solve a hard problem before the mainstream catches on.
4. **Anchor Cultural Affiliation ("People Like Us Do Things Like This"):**
   - Articulate the shared values of your community in one unmistakable sentence (e.g., *"Builders like us don't ship ghost listings, hidden trackers, or 500 MB dependencies—we ship clean, verified tools that work on any machine."*).
5. **Build "Selfish" Word-of-Mouth Mechanics into the Product:**
   - Design artifacts that naturally benefit the user *when they share them*:
     - Clean, embeddable badges, scorecards, or verified handoff receipts that prove the user's own high standards.
     - Collaborative features, starter templates, or checklists that only reach full power when shared with a teammate or client.

## Gotchas
- **Status does not mean elitism:** In technical and operational communities, status comes from **craft, reliability, and generosity**, not artificial scarcity or luxury posturing.
- **Fake urgency destroys trust:** Never use bogus countdown timers or fabricated waitlists; real tension comes from an honest look at an unsolved problem.
- **Word-of-mouth cannot be bribed:** Referral popups fail if sharing doesn't make the sharer look helpful and knowledgeable to their peers.
