# User Empathy Research Skill

## Summary
Uncover what real people actually do, fear, and value—replacing polite survey answers and founder wishful thinking with behavioral observation and an Actionable Empathy Brief.

## When to Use
- Before launching a product, feature, service, or campaign.
- When user signups, retention, or conversions stall despite positive survey feedback.
- When defining or narrowing your Smallest Viable Audience (SVA).

## Steps
1. **Separate the Three Decision Lenses:**
   - **End Consumer / Daily User:** The person physically clicking, operating, or experiencing the product every day (focus: friction, habit, relief).
   - **Buyer / Gatekeeper:** The person approving budget or institutional adoption (focus: risk reduction, compliance, defensibility).
   - **Shareholder / Stakeholder:** The executive, community, or partner measuring macro outcomes (focus: ROI, reputation, long-term alignment).
   - *Output:* A 3-row table stating who holds each lens and what makes them say "no."
2. **Design a Forced Empathy Cycle:**
   - **"Sit Next to the User" Observation:** Watch (or reconstruct step-by-step) a real user completing the job in their natural environment without coaching them.
   - **Micro-Frustration Logging:** Record exact moments of hesitation, tab-switching, workarounds, spreadsheets, or sighing.
   - **Binary-Choice & Fear-Based Probes:** Replace vague survey questions (*"Would you use this?"*) with concrete trade-offs (*"Would you give up X to guarantee Y never fails during an inspection?"* or *"What is the worst consequence if this goes wrong on a Tuesday morning?"*).
3. **Map the 4 Core Human Drivers:**
   - **Freedom from Fear:** What costly mistake, embarrassment, audit failure, or wasted time are they trying to prevent?
   - **Status:** How does using this make them look capable, prepared, or respected in front of peers or bosses?
   - **Affiliation:** Which group or professional identity does this connect them to?
   - **Convenience:** How many minutes, clicks, or mental steps does this eliminate right now?
4. **Run a Narrative-Mismatch Check:**
   - Compare **Founder Story** (*what we think they buy*) vs. **Customer Reality** (*what they actually pay or switch for*). Flag every gap where internal terminology clashes with the user's lived worldview.
5. **Lock the Smallest Viable Audience (SVA) & Output the Brief:**
   - Define the smallest, most specific group of people whose problem is so acute they will adopt immediately and tell peers.
   - Deliver a 1-page **Actionable Empathy Brief** containing: *3 Lenses, Top 3 Micro-Frustrations, 4 Driver Map, Narrative Mismatch Fix, and Locked SVA.*

## Gotchas
- **Never trust hypothetical compliments:** Politeness in interviews (*"That looks cool!"*) is not evidence of demand; look for existing painful workarounds or spent money/time.
- **Never blend the Buyer and the End User:** If a school director buys software that kitchen staff find slow, adoption dies on day two.
- **Avoid demographic fluff:** *"Ages 25–45 tech-savvy professionals"* is useless; *"Independent food operators who lose 3 hours a week checking city permit PDFs"* is actionable.
