# Category Positioning Audit Skill

## Summary
Rescue a product, repo, or service from commodity price wars by replacing generic "better/faster/cheaper" claims with sharp category edges, explicit exclusions, and undeniable proof assets.

## When to Use
- When prospects compare you solely on price or treat your offering as interchangeable with dozens of competitors.
- When rewriting a homepage, GitHub `README.md`, pitch deck, or service proposal.
- When entering a crowded market dominated by bloated incumbents.

## Steps
1. **Diagnose "The Cheap Pizza Problem" (Commodity Trap Audit):**
   - Inspect current copy and positioning. Highlight every generic claim that any competitor could paste onto their own site (*"high quality," "modern UI," "AI-powered," "customer-focused"*).
   - Identify where you are competing in a race to the bottom on price or feature checklists instead of owning a distinct point of view.
2. **Sharpen Meaningful Specifics:**
   - Replace every vague adjective with a concrete, verifiable mechanism or number (e.g., replace *"fast and lightweight"* with *"zero npm dependencies, `< 5 KB` bundle, auto-tunes to 2 GB RAM devices"*).
   - State the exact problem you solve, the exact mechanism you use, and the measurable result.
3. **Define Explicit Exclusion Boundaries ("Not For You / Go Down the Street"):**
   - Write 3 clear statements declaring who should **not** buy or use this offering (e.g., *"If you want a 400-feature enterprise suite with 6-month onboarding, go down the street. We build zero-bloat tools that ship in 60 seconds."*).
   - Use exclusion to build immediate trust with the right buyers by proving you refuse to dilute the product for everyone.
4. **Audit Experience vs. Credentials:**
   - Strip away passive credential padding (*certifications, buzzwords, years in business*) and elevate **lived operational proof** (*real bugs caught, real inspections passed, live interactive demos, open benchmarks*).
5. **Design Remarkable Proof Assets:**
   - Specify 1–3 self-evident proof assets that prospects can verify in under 30 seconds before paying or starring (e.g., a live interactive calculator/preview, an automated dead-link audit log, or a side-by-side teardown).

## Gotchas
- **Trying to please everyone pleases no one:** If your exclusion boundary doesn't turn away at least 50% of generic traffic, it is too soft.
- **Don't attack named competitors:** Contrast your specific approach against broken **industry categories** (e.g., *"Legacy Ad-Heavy Job Boards"* or *"Bloated Enterprise Monorepos"*), never competitor trademarks.
- **Claims without proof increase skepticism:** Never publish a bold positioning claim without pairing it immediately with a verifiable artifact or live demonstration.
