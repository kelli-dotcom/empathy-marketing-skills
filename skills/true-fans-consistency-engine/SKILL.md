# True Fans Consistency Engine Skill

## Summary
Build durable organic traction by earning 10, then 100, then 1,000 True Fans through dependable, high-signal output—eliminating burnout from algorithm chasing and procrastination disguised as "waiting for inspiration."

## When to Use
- When a founder, creator, or open-source builder feels stuck, overwhelmed by social algorithms, or tempted to pivot prematurely.
- When designing a sustainable publishing, shipping, or community-growth cadence.
- When launching a new repository, newsletter, or product from zero audience.

## Steps
1. **Audit Virality Traps vs. True-Fan Assets:**
   - Identify and cut low-leverage habits aimed at impressing strangers (engagement bait, trend-hopping, vanity metrics).
   - Focus 100% of effort on compounding assets that serve people who actually care about the craft.
2. **Map the 10 → 100 → 1,000 True Fans Ladder:**
   - **First 10 Fans (Direct Hand-to-Hand Value):** Name 10 specific people or communities experiencing the exact problem today. Deliver a tool, fix, or resource tailored directly to them and ask for honest friction feedback.
   - **Next 100 Fans (Repeatable Proof & Referrals):** Package the solution so the first 10 fans look smart sharing it with peers. Document every edge case they hit into public improvements.
   - **1,000 True Fans (Ecosystem & Standard):** Connect complementary tools, templates, and documentation into a cohesive ecosystem where every new release lifts all previous work.
3. **Separate Professional Consistency from Performative Authenticity:**
   - **Performative Authenticity (Avoid):** Oversharing personal drama, mood-dependent shipping, or making excuses when quality slips.
   - **Professional Consistency (Lock In):** Showing up on a predictable schedule with clean, tested, useful work that respects the audience's time—even on days when motivation is low.
4. **Lock the Minimum Viable Shipping Cadence:**
   - Define a non-negotiable weekly or bi-weekly deliverable (e.g., *1 verified feature release, 1 concrete teardown, or 1 open-source skill update*) that can be sustained for 52 weeks straight.
5. **Run the "Get Unstuck" Action Protocol:**
   - Whenever momentum stalls, shrink the scope immediately to three rules:
     1. **One Real Person:** Pick one actual user or operator with an urgent bottleneck.
     2. **One Real Problem:** Build or write the cleanest possible fix for that single bottleneck today.
     3. **Ship & Repeat:** Put the verified fix in their hands, record what worked, and repeat tomorrow.

## Gotchas
- **Never confuse motion with shipping:** Tweaking logos, reorganizing folders, or debating growth hacks does not count toward the consistency cadence—only shipped, user-facing value counts.
- **Don't quit during the quiet middle:** Trust compounds invisibly before it shows up in stars, referrals, or revenue.
- **Protect the quality floor:** Consistency means dependable excellence, never flooding channels with rushed AI filler.
