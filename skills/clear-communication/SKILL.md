# Clear Communication Skill

## Summary
Say what it is, plainly. Put the point first, then the detail. One point at a time. Short sentences. Nothing extra. The reader should know what you mean after the first sentence, and know what to do after the last one.

## The rules
1. The point first, then the detail. Lead with the answer, the decision, or the news. The detail comes after, only if the reader needs it to act.
2. Short sentences. One idea per sentence. If a sentence holds two ideas, split it.
3. One point at a time. Finish one point before starting the next. Do not stack points in one paragraph.
4. No rambling. Say it once. If a sentence does not change what the reader knows or does, cut it.
5. Do not avoid the point. Say the hard thing directly. Do not bury it, soften it into nothing, or walk around it.
6. No jargon unless the reader needs it. Use the reader's words, not the field's words. If a plain word works, use the plain word.
7. Say what it is, plainly. Name the thing. Do not hint at it, imply it, or make the reader guess.

## What clear looks like
- The first sentence tells the reader what this is about.
- Every sentence earns its place. Read it back and cut any sentence that repeats, hedges, or stalls.
- The reader can repeat the point back in one sentence.
- The reader knows what happens next, or what to do, without asking.

## What unclear looks like
- The point is buried three paragraphs down.
- Sentences run on, holding three ideas at once.
- The same thing said three ways, in case one of them lands.
- The hard thing is hinted at but never said.
- The reader finishes and still has to ask what you meant.

## Run it
1. Write the point in one sentence. If you cannot, you do not know the point yet. Find it first.
2. Put that sentence first.
3. Add only the detail the reader needs to act on the point. One point at a time.
4. Read it back. Cut every sentence that does not change what the reader knows or does.
5. Check the hard thing. Is it said directly? If not, say it.
6. Hand it over. If the reader can repeat the point back in one sentence, it is clear.
