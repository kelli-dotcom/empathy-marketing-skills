# Mistakes & Guardrails (`mistakes.md`)

## Never Repeat
1. **Polite Survey Bias:** Never mistake compliments in user interviews for willingness to switch or pay—always run the Forced Empathy Cycle (`skills/user-empathy-research/SKILL.md`) and observe real workarounds.
2. **Commodity Feature Checklists:** Never position an offering with generic adjectives (*"modern, fast, easy"*); always pair sharp specifics with explicit *"not for you"* exclusion boundaries (`skills/category-positioning-audit/SKILL.md`).
3. **Raw Unedited AI Dumps:** Never hand clients or users generic AI filler—keep AI as the high-speed intern and human empathy, taste, and accountability at the center (`skills/freelancer-to-premium-clients/SKILL.md`).
